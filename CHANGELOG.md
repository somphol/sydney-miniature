# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-08

Initial release.

- Two-shore Sydney Harbour geography: CBD and The Rocks, Barangaroo and Darling Harbour,
  Woolloomooloo, the Botanic Garden and Domain, Milsons Point/Kirribilli, North Sydney and Fort
  Denison island.
- Eight landmarks: Opera House, Harbour Bridge, Sydney Tower, Queen Victoria Building, St Mary's
  Cathedral, Luna Park, Fort Denison, Sydney Observatory.
- Nine ordinary building families and 268 parcels.
- Connected road graph with the Harbour Bridge as the only crossing; 428 road edges, 49 routes.
- Ferries, catamaran, yachts, cruise boat and tug; light rail, double-deck train, buses, taxis,
  cars, vans and 64 pedestrians.
- Day/night lighting, landmark camera flights, English UI, 345 × 345 embed.
- Plan/geometry tests and a 21-check deterministic browser gate.
- Single-file offline build and GitHub Pages release.
