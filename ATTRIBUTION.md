# Attribution

This is an original Sydney scene. It was built from scratch with the `city-miniature` skill's
method: road-first geography, authored landmarks and building families, a connected graph,
deterministic verification and a single-file release. No scene code, coordinates, landmarks, copy
or test targets were copied from another city miniature.

The mechanism (instanced primitive kit, virtual-time capture API, camera guard, single-file build)
is a general pattern documented by the skill and its San Francisco reference. The Sydney outline,
harbour, street names, landmark models, anchors, views, envelopes, palette, copy and verification
targets are new work.
